SLOB ANALYSIS (Slow / Obsolete Inventory)

A material is SLOB when it had zero consumption (negative quantity) from the 1st day of the month 6 months back until today (e.g. Mar 1 → Sep 13); SLOB value = sum of closing values of those items. All three SBUs source value from DataMart.inv.tblInventoryStatement (ClosingStock × AvgRate, latest per item/warehouse). RM/PM split is keyword-based.

SLOB ANALYSIS (iBOS LOGIC) — ClosingStock × itm.numAverageRate

Same SLOB definition (zero consumption from 1st of month 6 months back to today), but closing value = ClosingStock × itm.tblItemArc.numAverageRate (master rate, iBOS) instead of `DataMart AvgRate`. Use this to compare with the statement-based SLOB above. Example: Hessian Jute Bag 50kg (1111341) 151,063 × 55.5 = 83,83,996 (iBOS) vs 151,063 × 30.53 = 46,12,103 (statement).
